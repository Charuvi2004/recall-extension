# Recall privacy policy

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

Recall is a Chrome extension that helps students remember what they learn from YouTube playlists.

## What Recall stores

All of it is stored only in your browser (Chrome's local extension storage):

- **Courses you track:** playlist ID, playlist title, and each video's ID, title and duration.
- **Your progress:** how far you got in each tracked video.
- **Your flags:** video ID and title, the timestamp you flagged, and any note you typed.
- **Usage events:** for example "flag created" or "card reviewed", with a time, a random anonymous ID, and the related playlist, video or card ID.

## What Recall does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any server.
- It does not read any website other than youtube.com.
- It does not read your YouTube account, history, comments or subscriptions.
- It does not use cookies, ads or third-party analytics.

## Sharing

Data leaves your browser only if **you** choose to download your events file (Data → Download events.csv) and send it to someone, for example the organiser of a research pilot. That file contains usage events with your anonymous ID. It contains no video titles, notes or browsing history.

If the pilot organiser has set up an uninstall survey, uninstalling Recall opens a Google Form with your anonymous ID filled in. Submitting it is optional.

## Deleting your data

Open Recall → Data → **Delete all my Recall data**, or uninstall the extension. Either removes everything Recall stored.
